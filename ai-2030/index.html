AI 2030: Draw Your Future

The UK government sketched five futures for AI as shapes on six dials. Draw your own, place your bet, and find out where your group really disagrees.

In June 2026 the UK's Government Office for Science published five scenarios for where AI could stand in 2030, from a slow burn to a full take-off. Each scenario is drawn as a shape over six critical uncertainties: how capable AI gets, who holds it, whether it stays secure, how widely it's adopted, what it does to jobs, and whether nations cooperate.

The report's authors picked their five shapes. This page lets you draw yours.

1 · Draw your 2030

Where do you expect each dial to stand in 2030? Drag the corners of the shape, or tap anywhere along an axis. Each dial runs 1 to 5; touch one to see what its ends mean. On a keyboard, Tab to a corner and use the arrow keys.

2 · Place your bets

Which of these do you actually expect the world of 2030 to look like? Tap a card to read what each future means. Then split 100% of your confidence across the five: go all-in on one, or hedge. The official shape of your top pick appears dashed over your radar, so you can see how far your drawing strays from your bets.

3 · The group board

Everyone draws and bets on their own device. When you're done: type your name, tap "Copy my link", and post the link in the meeting chat. Then collect the others: copy their links from the chat and paste them below (all of them in one paste works). The board stays hidden until you reveal it, so you can collect the whole group before anyone peeks.

Sharing one device instead? "Add me" puts you on the board and clears the canvas for the next person.
